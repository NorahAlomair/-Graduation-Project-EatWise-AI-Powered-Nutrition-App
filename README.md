EatWise – AI-Powered Nutrition and Food Recognition Application



I worked on my graduation project, EatWise, an AI-powered mobile application designed to help users make healthier and more informed food choices. The application provides personalized meal recommendations, calorie tracking, grocery product recognition, and health alerts. It combines Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to analyze food-related information and deliver personalized nutrition insights to users.



🛠 Tools Used: Flutter, FastAPI, SQLite, Python, YOLOv8, CLIP



📌 Objective of the Project:



The project aims to support users in making healthier food choices by:



Providing personalized meal recommendations based on users' nutritional needs and preferences.

Tracking daily calorie intake and nutritional information.

Recognizing grocery products using Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision.

Providing health alerts based on food and nutritional information.

Integrating AI-generated outputs into a mobile application to provide useful and personalized nutrition insights.

Developing an easy-to-use mobile application that combines nutrition data with AI-powered features.



🤖 AI & Computer Vision:



The project integrates Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision techniques to support food and grocery product recognition. YOLOv8 was used as part of the object detection process, while CLIP was integrated to support image and food-related recognition tasks. The AI outputs were connected to the application to provide users with relevant information and personalized nutrition insights.
